# Governance

Toonlight is a free, open-source project that its community maintains together.

## Roles

- **Contributors**: anyone who opens an issue, answers a question, translates, tests, or sends a
  pull request.
- **Maintainers**: contributors who can review and merge pull requests and publish releases. The
  project founder is the first maintainer. A contributor with a record of good reviews and pull
  requests can be invited by the maintainers; maintainers who stay inactive for a year move to
  emeritus.

## How decisions are made

- Day to day: a pull request is merged when a maintainer approves it and the checks pass.
- Bigger changes (file format, new dependencies, the interface layout, the licence): open an issue
  first. We look for agreement in the discussion; if there is none, the maintainers decide by
  majority, and the founder breaks ties.
- Every decision follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Releases

Stable versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org). The steps are in
[`docs/RELEASING.md`](docs/RELEASING.md); every release lists its changes in
[`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).
